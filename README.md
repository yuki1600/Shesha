# Śeṣa (Shesha)

An invite-only, India-first mobile community app designed for people belonging to, and those interested in learning about, the Ramanuja Sampradaya. Built with a focus on trust, respect, and deep utility, Śeṣa provides a purposeful platform centered around four foundational pillars: **Dharma, Artha, Kama, and Moksha**.

## 📖 Overview

Śeṣa is designed as a calm, functional, and modern application available on iOS and Android. It avoids addictive mechanics, such as endless swiping or noisy feeds, and instead provides purposeful, Sampradaya-aligned networking, content, and opportunities. 

Privacy and trust are built-in from day one: the app is accessible by invite-only, limits exposure of personal contact data, and ensures interactions are heavily opt-in. The experience is fully localized with native support for both English and Tamil.

---

## 🏛️ The Four Pillars

The app experience is divided into four main areas, each solving a clear job to be done for the community:

### 1. 📿 Dharma (Service & Practice)
Focuses on service-oriented participation and day-to-day spiritual practice.
- **Seva & Announcements:** Browse and sign up for volunteer (seva) opportunities and temple/community events.
- **Event Tracking:** Simple intention tracking to mark "Interested" or "Going" to community gatherings.
- **Private Checklist:** A secure, private daily practice checklist customized to personal spiritual development (never shared publicly).

### 2. 💼 Artha (Professional Discovery)
A credible community jobs and candidate discovery hub with lightweight, respectful contact workflows to help community members grow together professionally.
- **Job Listings:** Post and discover jobs featuring skills, location, workplace mode, and requirements.
- **Candidate Cards:** Users can signal they are "open to work" with dedicated candidate summaries.
- **Structured Introductions:** No spammy open DMs. Contact details are only shared after a formal "intro request" is approved by the recipient.

### 3. 🌺 Kama (Matchmaking)
Enabling respectful, profile-first matchmaking with controlled discovery and explicit contact approvals.
- **Secure Profiles:** Rich profiles capturing biodata, values, and practices seamlessly. These profiles are isolated and only visible within the Kama pillar.
- **Guardian Integration:** Optional integration for users who wish to include a guardian in mutual introductions.
- **Double Opt-In Contact:** Expressing interest creates a mutual match state. Phone numbers or emails are strictly kept private until both parties consent to a structured contact request.

### 4. 🕉️ Moksha (Spiritual Feed & Q&A)
A calm, content-driven spiritual feed for consuming knowledge, saving items, and engaging in simple Q&A.
- **Curated Feed:** Browse link and text posts natively tagged by topic, teacher, and language.
- **Knowledge Sharing:** Dedicated Q&A post types for community inquiries.
- **Engagement:** Save features for later reading and flat comment sections for productive discussions.

---

## 🛡️ Core Principles

- **Calm over Noisy:** Simple, restrained layouts. No addictive mechanics.
- **Respectful by Default:** Strict privacy rules. Users have full control over what personal information is visible.
- **Utility First:** Real tools for real community needs (jobs, matchmaking, volunteering).
- **Deep Localization:** UI and content are thoughtfully designed for both Tamil and English speakers from the ground up.
- **Safety Centric:** Backed by robust reporting/blocking flows, rate limiting, and an admin moderation console to secure the community.
